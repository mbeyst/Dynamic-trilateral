## Video Script Template (60 seconds)

```
OPENING (0-5 sec)
─────────────────
[Character + Situation]
"A Spanish executive was..."
"One of my Clarity House students..."
"I emailed a CEO this week..."

PROBLEM (5-20 sec)
──────────────────
[Concrete detail, not abstraction]
"Three children. One company. Traditional succession would name one heir."
"€5M building expansion. Senior pastor strongly in favor. No one said anything."

SHIFT (20-40 sec)
─────────────────
[What DTL would do differently]
"Here's what would happen with a triad..."
"Recorded dissent would have..."
"Dynamic authority means..."

RESULT/QUESTION (40-60 sec)
───────────────────────────
[Concrete outcome OR question for audience]
"Five years later: no departure of passed-over heirs."
"18 months later: attendance growth stalled at 3%."
"Tell me: would this work in your organization?"

CTA (last 5 sec)
────────────────
"Follow the journey: dynamictrilateral.org"
"Clarity House students: email me if you want to pilot this"
"Comment: am I missing something?"
```

## Production Workflow

### Batch Recording (2 hours/week)

**Option A: Record 7 videos in one session**
- Sunday afternoon, 2 hours
- Same outfit, same location, same lighting
- Record all 7 videos back-to-back
- Schedule posts for the week (LinkedIn native scheduler)

**Option B: Record every other day**
- 30 minutes per session
- Record 2-3 videos at once
- More responsive to current events
- Less batch fatigue

### Setup (one-time, 30 minutes)

```
Location: Same spot every time
──────────────────────────────
- Face a window (natural light from front)
- Plain wall behind you (no distractions)
- Phone on a stand (landscape mode)
- Test audio (phone mic is fine, just check for echo)

Tech Stack (free/cheap)
───────────────────────
- Recording: Phone camera (1080p, 30fps)
- Editing: CapCut (free) or skip editing entirely
- Captions: CapCut auto-captions (free)
- Scheduling: LinkedIn native scheduler (free)
- Thumbnail: Canva (free tier)

File Naming Convention
──────────────────────
YYYY-MM-DD_DTL_[topic].mp4
Example: 2026-07-15_DTL_church-board-story.mp4

Storage
───────
C:\Users\martinb\dynamic-trilateral\videos\2026\07-July\
```

### The 15-Minute Recording Session

```
Minute 0-2: Setup
─────────────────
- Phone on stand
- Check lighting
- Test audio (record 5 seconds, playback)

Minute 2-5: Script Review
─────────────────────────
- Read the script once
- Identify key phrases (don't memorize, just know the beats)
- Decide: one take or two?

Minute 5-12: Record
───────────────────
- Hit record
- Do the script (60 seconds)
- Pause 3 seconds before stopping (gives editing room)
- If you flub: pause 3 seconds, restart that section
  (easier to cut in editing than to redo entire video)

Minute 12-15: Review & Save
───────────────────────────
- Watch once (don't perfectionist it)
- Save with correct filename
- Move to editing (or skip editing and schedule)
```

### Editing (Optional, 5 minutes/video)

**If you have time:**
1. Import to CapCut
2. Trim dead air at start/end
3. Auto-captions (review for accuracy)
4. Export 1080p

**If you don't have time:**
- Skip editing
- Upload raw to LinkedIn
- LinkedIn auto-generates captions (not perfect, but functional)

## Repurposing System

### One Video → Five Pieces of Content

```
Original: 60-second LinkedIn video
         ↓
1. LinkedIn text post (same hook, expand to 3 paragraphs)
2. Twitter/X thread (hook + 3 tweets breaking it down)
3. Email newsletter snippet (for Clarity House list)
4. Instagram Reel (same video, different caption)
5. Website update (add to journey timeline)
```

### Monthly Compilation

End of each month:
- Combine top 4 videos into one "Month in Review" video
- Post on website + LinkedIn
- Shows progress, not just daily fragments

## Burnout Prevention

### The "Skip Without Guilt" Rules

1. **Sunday is always off** — No exceptions
2. **If you're sick, batch next week early** — Don't try to record while unwell
3. **If a topic isn't landing, pivot** — Don't force dead content
4. **If you miss a day, don't double up** — Just continue. One gap doesn't break trust.

### The "Good Enough" Standard

```
Perfectionist:  "I need to redo that take, my hair looked weird"
Sustainable:    "That take was fine. The message matters more."

Perfectionist:  "I need better lighting, better mic, better editing"
Sustainable:    "Phone + window + honest message = enough"

Perfectionist:  "I'll post when I have something groundbreaking"
Sustainable:    "I'll post when I have something honest"
```

### The Quarterly Break

Every 3 months:
- Take one full week off
- Announce it in advance ("Back [date]")
- Repost top performers during the break
- Come back with fresh energy

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| "I don't know what to post tomorrow" | Check the content calendar. If stuck, do a "Friday Question" format any day. |
| "My videos feel repetitive" | Good. Repetition reinforces the message. Vary the stories, not the framework. |
| "No one is commenting" | Ask better questions. End with "Tell me if I'm wrong" not "Thoughts?" |
| "I'm running out of time" | Skip editing. Record while walking. Do voice-only one day. |
| "A video flopped" | Delete it from your mind. Tomorrow's video is a fresh start. |
| "Someone criticized the framework" | Perfect. Make a video about it. "A viewer told me I'm wrong about X..." |
