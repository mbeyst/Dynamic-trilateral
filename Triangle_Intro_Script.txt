Triangle - Intro Video Script
==============================

As an organisation...
how do we make the best decisions?

[PAUSE]

Better technology?
Better people?
Or better structure?

[PAUSE]

Most solutions focus on either AI or personal development.
Very few consider structure.

[PAUSE]

What if we humble ourselves and learn from Creation and God?

[PAUSE]

This is what Triangle is about.

[PAUSE]

God gave us the blueprint.
Yet somehow we have refused to listen.

[PAUSE]

No one as king or permanent leader.
But rather a structure that allows us to Mimic the Trinity.

[PAUSE]

AI facilitates what God designed.

[PAUSE]

Here's how it works:

Three people per triangle.
Everyone speaks.

[PAUSE]

Two agree — decision moves.
One dissents — dissent preserved.
Dissent travels with the decision.

[PAUSE]

Humans decide.
The system learns.

[PAUSE]

Who is best in what context.
When difference leads to insight.
Who makes the best decisions.

[PAUSE]

Over time, authority morphs.
Flowing to competence.
Not title. Not tenure.
Competence.

[PAUSE]

The system adapts.
It remembers.
It improves.

[PAUSE]

Triangles coordinate with other triangles.

One triangle decides locally.
Multiple triangles agree — organization-wide.
The more triangles agree — the more authority it carries.

[PAUSE]

I'm building it now.

Pre-revenue. Pre-pilot. Documenting everything publicly.

[PAUSE]

I'm looking for two people to form the first triangle.
Not employees.
Partners.

[PAUSE]

Who will be first?

[PAUSE]

Every voice has value —
follow the journey.
Become part of it.
