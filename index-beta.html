Triangle

Beta

Structured, Accountable, Humane Decision-Making

Triangle is an organizational structure where all significant decisions are made by three-person cells with recorded dissent, dynamic authority based on competence rather than title, and AI as advisory only.
Full transparency: This framework is new. It synthesizes existing ideas (Simmel's triad theory, Social Trinitarianism, servant leadership) into operational mechanisms that haven't been tested together. We're seeking pilot organizations to implement this alongside us and help refine it through real-world use.

The Four Mechanisms

1. The Triad as Minimum Stable Unit

No person should lead alone. Each decision cell consists of three people. Small enough for accountability. Large enough for disagreement.

2. 2/3 Voting with Recorded Dissent

Unanimity is preferred. When it cannot be reached, a 2/3 decision may proceed — but dissent is recorded and travels with the decision.

3. Dynamic Authority

Authority shifts based on proximity to facts, track record, and vindicated dissent — not static title.

4. AI as Advisory Only

AI recommends. Humans decide. Everything is logged. Responsibility stays human.

Where This Came From

This framework synthesizes: Georg Simmel (triad stability, 1890s), Social Trinitarians (Moltmann, Volf, LaCugna — Trinity as relational model), Robert Greenleaf (servant leadership, 1970s), and distributed leadership research (2000s). The operational mechanisms (2/3 voting, systematic dissent recording, dynamic authority, AI advisory) are new. Full lineage documentation will be published after pilot learnings are incorporated.
