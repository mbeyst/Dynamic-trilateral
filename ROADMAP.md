## Phase 2: Pilot Organizations (Months 2-6)

**Goal:** Run 3 pilot programs, generate case studies, prove the model works

### 2.1 Recruit Pilot Organizations
**Target:** 3 organizations (church, nonprofit, Christian business)
**Criteria:**
- 20-100 employees (large enough to matter, small enough to move)
- Leadership open to experimentation
- Willing to document the journey publicly
**Offer:** Free/low-cost (€5k-10k for 3 months)
**Action:**
- Use website contact forms
- Leverage LinkedIn network
- Ask warm introductions (Clarity House students)
**Owner:** Martin
**Status:** Not started

### 2.2 Deliver Pilots
**What:**
- Train triads (2/3 voting, recorded dissent, dynamic authority)
- Monthly check-ins
- Collect data (decisions made, dissent recorded, satisfaction)
**Metrics:**
- Decision speed (before vs. after)
- Dissent rate (how often people disagree on record)
- Leader burnout (self-reported)
- Succession readiness (can the org function without the top person?)
**Owner:** Martin
**Status:** Not started

### 2.3 Document Results
**What:** Case studies, testimonials, data
**Format:** Written reports, video interviews, public blog posts
**Use:** Grant applications, donor updates, marketing
**Owner:** Martin
**Status:** Not started

## Notes

**Funding First:** Everything else depends on this. Without runway, pilots don't happen, proof isn't generated, scale is impossible.

**Honesty About Stage:** Don't oversell. "Pre-revenue, pre-pilot, building the case" is the honest pitch. The right people will back the journey, not just the results.

**Dual Track:** Theological framing for Christian orgs, sociological/management framing for secular. Same structure, different language.

**80/20 Split:** Keep Clarity House running (80% content, 20% DTL) until DTL revenue replaces it. Don't burn the bridge.
